Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

QuiZee is a test and progress-tracking platform for JEE students. Tests are set by the coaching or teacher who runs this QuiZee site (“the administrator”). This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password. If you use Sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your test activity: the answers you give, time spent per question, scores, ranks, and autosaved answers during a test.
  • Practice and progress: your mistake notebook, practice sets, target marks, daily goal, streak and badges.
  • Technical data: your login session is kept in your browser’s local storage. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in, take tests and see your results, rank, percentile and progress over time.
  • To show the administrator your results so they can help the batch improve.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as a password-reset code.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything unrelated to your learning on QuiZee.

Google user data

When you choose Sign in with Google, QuiZee uses your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create or open your QuiZee account. We don’t access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data, and we don’t share Google user data with anyone. QuiZee’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services we rely on

Your data is stored and processed by these providers, only to run QuiZee: Vercel (website), Render (server), Neon (database), Brevo (password-reset emails), Google (sign-in), Cloudinary (question images, if enabled) and OpenRouter (AI chapter tags and explanations, which receive question text only, never your personal details).

Who can see your data

You see your own results. The administrator can see results and progress for students on their site. Other students see only leaderboard information such as name, rank and score.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. To correct or delete your account and data, ask the administrator of your QuiZee site; they can remove it, and it is then deleted from our database.

Security

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, connections use HTTPS, and answer keys are never sent to students before they submit a test.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
